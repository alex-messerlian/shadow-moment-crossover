\textbf{The readout sensitivity check is a simulation.} It uses a single
readout model, the measured per-qubit rates of Section 6.3 and a uniform
6.5\% comparator, with independent and time-invariant readout and no
correlated or drifting component; and the collective route's gate, depth,
and routing overhead is not folded into its RMSE. It bounds the direction
of the crossover shift, not its location on hardware.

We then test the criterion on what the platform reports as a 108-qubit
superconducting processor, with the predictions committed to version
control beforehand. Both failed: a Bell-state purity locked at \(0.89\)
measured \(0.72\), a single-copy anchor locked at \(1.50\) measured
\(1.34\), intervals disjoint. We do not demonstrate the crossover on
hardware; the informative part is that static, single-session
characterization did not predict collective-measurement performance on the
platform and execution path we tested.
